# Arthion dew and potion recipes

The unchanged `recipes-reference.png` was supplied by the user on 26 October 2026. Its twelve recipes are transcribed left to right, top row first. All output quantities, ingredient counts and Yang fees come from that Arthion screen, rather than generic Metin2 crafting tables. Each recipe produces ten items. No success rates, durations or extra empty-bottle requirements have been inferred.

The user confirmed that the custom coloured bottles can be labelled Cyan, Purple and Black; the second white bottle uses the descriptive colour label White. These stay separate from White Dew and the standard Purple Potion. The user identified the yellow herb as Dandelion and the green, two-headed herb as Thistle.

Other herb/potion names were identified by matching original item icons against official Gameforge Metin2 Wiki images. The custom outputs, Dandelion and Thistle retain CSS windows into the supplied screenshot. Other icons are unchanged original Wiki assets. `ICON-SOURCES.json` records consulted pages/image URLs; `NAME-SOURCES.json` records official interlanguage titles. Missing interlanguage names use ordinary translated plant/colour labels.

The six standard dew names are Red Dew, Orange Dew, Yellow Dew, Green Dew, Blue Dew and White Dew. The final two recipes are Purple Potion and Green Potion. Original Wiki recipe requirements were not imported because Arthion's supplied recipes differ.
