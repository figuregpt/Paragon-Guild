# Boss artwork references

Artwork is original Metin2 monster artwork from Gameforge Wiki or images supplied by the user. Full references are listed in SOURCES.json. Artwork is hosted locally, without recolouring or generating substitute images.

Magma Golem, Dark Crystal Golem and Plague Carrier now use the exact images supplied by the user on 26 October 2026. Arthion's other custom variants (Ember Tiger, Thornspike Stump, Nightmare Tree and Ghost Forest Giant Ghost Tree) retain reference model artwork. The user requested removal of the visible representative-artwork badges; provenance remains recorded here and in SOURCES.json.

The four bosses added from the new chest references (Grim Reaper, Nine Tails, Yellow Tiger Ghost and Queen Spider) show standard map references from their official Wiki pages. Their exact Arthion spawn points are pending; no points were copied from generic Wiki spawn tables. They remain separate bosses; Yellow Tiger is not merged with Ember Tiger.

Direct drops from generic Wiki boss pages were not imported.
